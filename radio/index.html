    Finding a station’s link
    1. On the station’s website, look for Listen in other apps, Stream URL or Direct link. Right-click it and choose Copy link.
    2. If the link ends in .pls or .m3u, download that file, open it in a text editor, and copy the line that starts with https://.
    3. Still stuck? Play the station on its website, press F12, open Network, choose Media, and copy the address that appears.
    4. radio-browser.info lists stream links for thousands of stations.

    The link should start with https://.
