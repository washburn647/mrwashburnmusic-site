Lesson pages, offline test copy
================================

Open "Lesson pages.html" (double-click). No server or internet needed.
Use Chrome or Edge.

- Students, pieces and the library are saved in this browser, on this computer,
  separately from the website's studio. To bring students over from the website,
  press Backup there and Restore here (and the other way round).
- "Open page" shows the student's page from this folder.
- "Copy link" gives a mrwashburnmusic.com link. Those links only show pieces,
  flash cards and the rest once this version is on the website, so don't send
  them to families yet.
- Student codes can be made here, but Publish and Results need the website.

Pieces: MusicXML files (.musicxml, .xml or compressed .mxl), exported from
MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico, Noteflight or flat.io.
