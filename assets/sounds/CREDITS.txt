Electric guitar, electric piano, organ, ukulele, kalimba and the synths (electric-guitar/,

  banks from the FreePats project, https://freepats.zenvoid.org/

  - Electric piano: FM Synthesized Piano #1 (DX7 "E. Piano 1", recorded from Hexter)

  - Ukulele: recorded by Mateusz Dąbrowski
  - Kalimba: recorded by Xavimart, Gonzalo and Roberto
  - Synth lead: Synth Square; synth pad: Sweep Pad; synth bass: Lately Bass by Yingchun Soul
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Trimmed, retuned where measured off, converted to mono.
