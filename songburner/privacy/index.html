Privacy

The short version

Song Burner keeps your songs on your own device. Nothing is sold, there are no ads, and nothing tracks you. Signing in is only for beta testers, who must be 13 or older.

Without signing in

Your songs, scores and settings are saved in this browser on this device, and nowhere else. Clearing the site’s data in your browser deletes them.

If you sign in (beta testers)

The server keeps:

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • A copy of your songs, scores and settings, so they sync between your devices.
  • For Mr. Washburn, who runs the beta: your email, when you joined and last used it, and how many songs and practice runs you have. He does not see your songs or scores.

Settings > Account > Delete my cloud copy removes your copy from the server at any time.

If you apply for the beta

Your email address is sent to Mr. Washburn so he can add you. It is deleted once he has dealt with it, or once you sign in.

Other services

  • The site runs on Cloudflare, which also handles the sign-in code sent to your email.
  • Lyrics search sends the song title and artist you type to lrclib.net and lyrics.ovh. Nothing else about you goes with it.

Questions or deleting your data

Ask Mr. Washburn.
